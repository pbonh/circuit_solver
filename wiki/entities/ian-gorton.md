## Overview

Ian Gorton is a software architect, computer-science professor, and consultant with 30 years' experience in distributed and data-intensive systems. He is the author of Foundations of Scalable Systems (O'Reilly, 2022), Essential Software Architecture (Springer), and Data Intensive Computing (Cambridge University Press), along with 200+ scientific and professional publications.

## Characteristics

- PhD from Sheffield Hallam University, UK.
- Senior member of the IEEE Computer Society.
- Joined Northeastern University as a CS professor in 2015 (Seattle campus).
- Previously led R&D in big data and massively scalable systems at the CMU Software Engineering Institute.
- Worked on large-scale systems in banking, telecommunications, government, healthcare, and scientific modeling.

## Common Strategies

- Teaches the CS6650 "Building Scalable Distributed Systems" course at Northeastern.
- Emphasizes trade-off-driven architectural thinking and empirical performance studies.
