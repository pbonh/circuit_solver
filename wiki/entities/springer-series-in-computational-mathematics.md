## Overview

The "Springer Series in Computational Mathematics" is a numerical-analysis book series published by Springer-Verlag (Berlin Heidelberg GmbH). According to the front matter of Hairer-Wanner Vol. II, its editorial board is:

- R.L. Graham, La Jolla (CA)
- J. Stoer, Würzburg
- R. Varga, Kent (Ohio)

## Characteristics

- Volume 14 of the series is "Solving Ordinary Differential Equations II: Stiff and Differential-Algebraic Problems" by E. Hairer and G. Wanner (2nd revised edition 1996, corrected 2nd printing 2002; Mathematics Subject Classification 65Lxx, 34A50). ISBN 978-3-642-05220-0 (978-3-642-05221-7 ebook).
- The cover design "is based on Figure 10A on page 149 of this book" — a Wanner order-star figure, signature of the series' artistic style.
- Cover design: design&production, Heidelberg; camera-ready copy by the authors (TeX); printed on acid-free paper.

## Common Strategies

- Publishes graduate-textbook-density monographs in numerical analysis (ODE/PDE solvers, optimisation, sparse-matrix methods).
