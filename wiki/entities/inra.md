## Overview

INRA is the French National Institute for Agricultural Research, identified as one of the world's elite agricultural research institutes. INRA chose VLE (Virtual Laboratory Environment) to integrate its existing stock of agricultural simulation models and to develop new DEVS-based capabilities through the RECORD project.

## Characteristics

- World-leading agricultural research institute (France)
- Large stock of legacy agricultural simulation models
- Strong commitment to model reuse and collaborative development
- Selected DEVS/VLE after comparative evaluation of commercial and academic options

## Common Strategies

- RECORD project: documented rationale for choosing DEVS/VLE
- Model coupling across crop, weather, soil, and management formalisms
- Collaboration with model developers across French and international institutes
