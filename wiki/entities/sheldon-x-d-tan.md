## Overview

Sheldon X.-D. Tan is a professor at the University of California, Riverside and co-author of Advanced Symbolic Analysis for VLSI Systems. He led the chapters on DDDs, performance bound analysis, and GPU-parallel Monte Carlo statistical analysis.

## Characteristics

- Heads the MSLAB research group at UC Riverside.
- Long-running collaboration with NSF (CCF-1116882, CCF-1017090, OISE-1130402) and UC MEXUS-CONACYT.
- Research interests: symbolic analysis, statistical CAD, GPU-accelerated simulation.

## Common Strategies

- DDD-based statistical and worst-case analog analysis.
- Massively parallel DDD evaluation on GPU.
