## Overview

The University of Waterloo (Waterloo, Ontario, Canada) is the academic home of Jiri Vlach (Electrical Engineering) and Kishore Singhal (Systems Design), the authors of *Computer Methods for Circuit Analysis and Design*. Waterloo also developed WATAND (Waterloo Analysis and Design), the interactive CAD package used to produce many examples in the book.

## Characteristics

- Departments cited in the book: Electrical Engineering and Systems Design.
- Faculty acknowledged in the preface include H. K. Kesavan, S. G. Chamberlain, and P. R. Bryant.
- Site of teaching the book's material in fourth-year undergraduate and graduate electrical engineering courses for several years prior to publication.

## Common Strategies

- Industrial-academic collaboration in CAD tool development (WATAND).
- Pedagogy combining theoretical circuit theory with numerical analysis and programming.
