## Overview

Sebastian Reich is a numerical analyst whose 1996 papers on symplectic integration of constrained Hamiltonian systems and on higher-order semi-explicit symplectic partitioned Runge-Kutta methods are the foundation of Sect. VII.8 of Hairer-Wanner Vol. II.

## Characteristics

- Ascher, Chin & Reich (1994) — "Stabilization of DAEs and invariant manifolds" — joint paper with U.M. Ascher.
- Reich (1996a) — "Symplectic integration of constrained Hamiltonian systems by composition methods" — the composition-method theory used to lift symplectic integrators to manifolds.
- Reich (1996b) — "On higher-order semi-explicit symplectic partitioned Runge-Kutta methods" — produces high-order [[concepts/symplectic-method]]s for the constrained Hamiltonian setting.

## Common Strategies

- Composition methods to build higher-order symplectic integrators from second-order base steps.
- Semi-explicit symplectic partitioned RK for systems where part of the state is non-stiff.

## Related Entities

- U.M. Ascher, R. Chin — co-authors of the 1994 stabilization paper.
- [[entities/laurent-jay]] — counterpart on symplectic partitioned RK for constrained Hamiltonian systems.
