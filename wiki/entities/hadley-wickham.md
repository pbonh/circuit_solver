## Overview

Hadley Wickham is the statistician who formalized the split-apply-combine methodology and the principles of tidy data. The chapter cites his papers as the conceptual basis for Pandas' grouping API and tidy-data conventions.

## Characteristics

- Author of tidy-data and split-apply-combine papers
- R-language ecosystem contributor (tidyverse)
- Influential in data-science methodology

## Common Strategies

- Promote tidy structures before analysis
- Use grouped operations rather than imperative loops
