## Overview

Physics of Semiconductor Devices by S. M. Sze and Kwok K. Ng is the canonical graduate-level textbook and reference work for semiconductor device physics. The third edition (Wiley, 2006) reorganizes and updates the second edition (1981) into five parts covering semiconductor physics, device building blocks, transistors, negative-resistance and power devices, and photonic devices and sensors. With more than 15,000 citations at publication, it is one of the most-cited works in engineering and applied science.

## Characteristics

- Authors: Simon Min Sze (National Chiao Tung University; National Nano Device Laboratories, Hsinchu, Taiwan) and Kwok K. Ng (Agere Systems / MVC, San Jose).
- Publisher: John Wiley & Sons, 2006.
- Predecessors: 1st edition 1969, 2nd edition 1981.
- Structure: 14 chapters in 5 parts; problem sets and solutions manual.
- Coverage: classical p-n junctions, Schottky and MIS capacitors, BJTs, MOSFETs (including 3-D and nonvolatile memory), JFET/MESFET/MODFET, tunnel and resonant-tunneling diodes, IMPATT/BARITT/TUNNETT, transferred-electron devices, thyristors / IGBTs, LEDs and lasers, photodetectors and solar cells, thermal/mechanical/magnetic/chemical sensors.

## Common Strategies

- Used as both textbook (advanced undergraduate and graduate courses) and bench reference for device-design engineers and researchers.
- Frequently cited for material parameters, device equations, and historical references.
