## Overview

Anatolii B. Vasil'eva (Vasileva in transliteration) is a Russian mathematician whose 1963 paper systematized the asymptotic-expansion theory for [[concepts/singular-perturbation-problem]]s, including the construction of inner and outer expansions with boundary-layer terms. Hairer-Wanner Vol. II opens its "Expansions with Boundary Layer Terms" subsection of Sect. VI.2 with a Vasil'eva (1963) epigraph and credits her analysis as the foundation.

## Characteristics

- "These expansions were then analyzed by Vasil'eva (1963)" (Sect. VI.2). Her theory underpins the book's treatment of the inner and outer solutions and the corresponding boundary-layer corrections.
- Co-author with A.N. Tikhonov and A.G. Sveshnikov of "Differential Equations" (Translated 1985), one of the three "classical books on this subject" cited by Hairer-Wanner alongside Wasow (1965) and O'Malley (1974).

## Common Strategies

- Matched asymptotic expansions for singular-perturbation ODEs of the form `εy' = f(y, z), z' = g(y, z)`.
- Boundary-layer correction series whose terms decay outside an O(ε) initial layer.
