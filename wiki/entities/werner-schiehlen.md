## Overview

Werner Schiehlen is the German engineering scientist who edited "Multibody Systems Handbook" (Springer-Verlag, Berlin, 1990) — the source from which Hairer-Wanner Vol. II copies Fig. 7.1 (the seven-body Andrews' squeezer mechanism) and Tables 7.1 / 7.2 (its physical parameters) for the central benchmark of Sect. VII.7.

## Characteristics

- Schiehlen, ed. (1990) — Multibody Systems Handbook, the reference work cited as the source of "Andrews' squeezer mechanism" — 7 rigid bodies connected by friction-less joints, driven by a motor with constant drive torque mom = 0.033.
- The book attributes to Schiehlen (1990) the standardised numerical constants, geometry, and figure for the seven-body benchmark used to compare HEM5/HEX5/PHEM56/MEXX/RADAU5/RODAS.
- Schiehlen's epigraph (p. 7 of Sect. VII.7): the multibody-systems area encompasses biomechanics, robotics, vehicle design, and machinery dynamics.
