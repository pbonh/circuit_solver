## Overview

MS4 Me is the first commercial DEVS modeling-and-simulation product, available at http://ms4systems.com. It targets a wide audience — students, managers, modelers, developers, programmers — and lets each user work at their preferred level of formality. MS4 Me uses Finite Deterministic DEVS (FDDEVS) as the basis for a simplified, syntax/content-assisted natural-language style of DEVS modeling that beginners and non-programmers can adopt quickly.

## Characteristics

- Built on FDDEVS for accessible model authoring
- Natural-language statements to define DEVS atomic models and SES couplings
- Sequence Designer tool generates FDDEVS models plus an SES from sequential diagrams
- Java-based depth extensions for expert users
- Commercial product from MS4 Systems

## Common Strategies

- Beginners author models with natural-language assistive editor
- Experts extend FDDEVS skeletons with Java-coded DEVS depth
- Sequence Designer used to bootstrap initial models from sequence diagrams
- Used as the primary teaching/illustration environment in Chapters 2–12 of the Zeigler/Sarjoughian guide
