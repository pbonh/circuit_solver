## Overview

OpenAlea is a software platform for functional-structural plant modeling. It provides 3D plant-architecture tools, energy-balance computation, and microclimate feedback support. In the Zeigler/Sarjoughian VLE case study (Soulie et al. 2010), OpenAlea is coupled with the EcoMeristem model of rice morphogenesis to analyze drought tolerance and early-vigour phenotypes.

## Characteristics

- Functional-structural plant model platform
- 3D plant-architecture rendering
- Energy-balance and microclimate computation
- Python-based scientific software ecosystem

## Common Strategies

- Coupling with VLE-hosted DEVS models for crop simulation
- Integrated phenotype analysis across genotype × environment combinations
- Used in rice and sorghum drought-tolerance research
