## Overview

Syvert P. Nørsett is the Norwegian numerical analyst who, with Hairer and Wanner, co-authored Vol. I of "Solving Ordinary Differential Equations" (front-matter: "Bd.1 verf. von E. Hairer, S.P. Nørsett und G. Wanner"). The Vol. II preface lists him alongside Butcher and Dahlquist as the three "for their interest in the subject and for the numerous discussions we had with them which greatly inspired our work."

## Characteristics

- Nørsett (1974) — first construction of semi-explicit Runge-Kutta methods (Trondheim Report 6174); multiple Padé approximations to the exponential function — foundational results used throughout Sect. IV.4.
- Nørsett (1975) — Runge-Kutta methods with coefficients depending on the Jacobian; C-polynomials for rational approximations. Theorem 3.10 of Sect. IV.3 (the structure of SDIRK stability functions) is jointly attributed to K. Wright (1970) and Nørsett (1975).
- Nørsett (1976) — Runge-Kutta methods with a multiple real eigenvalue only (BIT 16) — the SIRK construction.
- Nørsett & Wolfbrandt (1977, 1979) — order conditions for Rosenbrock-type methods (Sect. IV.7).
- Nørsett & Wanner (1979) — the real-pole sandwich for rational approximations (Theorem 4.23 of Sect. IV.4); perturbed collocation (1981).
- Wanner, Hairer & Nørsett (1978) — order stars and stability theorems; "When I-stability implies A-stability" (Sect. IV.4).
- Iserles & Nørsett (1984) — a proof of the first Dahlquist barrier by order stars.

## Common Strategies

- Padé approximation theory for stability functions of one-step methods.
- Order-stars technique (jointly with Wanner and Iserles).
- Rosenbrock-method order conditions (jointly with Wolfbrandt).
