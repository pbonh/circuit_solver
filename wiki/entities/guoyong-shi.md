## Overview

Guoyong Shi is a professor at the School of Microelectronics, Shanghai Jiao Tong University and a co-author of Advanced Symbolic Analysis for VLSI Systems. He led the book's chapters on BDD foundations, layered expansion diagrams (LED), generalized two-graph theory, GPDD, hierarchical analysis, and symbolic moment computation.

## Characteristics

- Heads the Mixed-Signal Design Automation (MSDA) Laboratory at SJTU.
- NSFC grants 60572028, 60876089, 61176129 funded much of the work.
- Research interests: symbolic analog CAD, BDD-based methods, hierarchical analysis.

## Common Strategies

- Compact graph-based symbolic generation via BDD/GPDD.
- Hierarchical multi-port symbolic characterization.
