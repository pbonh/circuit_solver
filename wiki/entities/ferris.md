## Overview

Ferris is the unofficial mascot of the Rust programming language community: a small orange crab. In *The Rust Programming Language* book, Ferris is used as a visual annotation marking code samples that intentionally do not compile, that panic, or that do not produce the desired behavior.

## Characteristics

- Orange cartoon crab
- Used as an annotation in Rust learning materials
- Community-adopted, not officially trademarked by the Rust Foundation
- Three states in the book: does-not-compile, panics, wrong-behavior
