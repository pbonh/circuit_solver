## Overview

Olavi Nevanlinna is the Finnish numerical analyst whose 1976-1985 papers develop the logarithmic-norm and multiplier techniques used in Sects. IV.11 and V.6 of Hairer-Wanner Vol. II to prove contractivity for stiff differential equations. The preface lists him among the readers who provided "invaluable suggestions and remarks" on particular sections.

## Characteristics

- Nevanlinna (1976a) — logarithmic norms of a matrix (Report HTKK-MAT-A94, Helsinki Univ. of Technology) — the foundation of the [[concepts/logarithmic-norm]] section IV.11 builds on.
- Nevanlinna (1976b) — error bounds for G-stable methods.
- Nevanlinna (1977) — numerical integration of nonlinear initial value problems.
- Nevanlinna (1985) — matrix-valued versions of a result of von Neumann.
- Nevanlinna & Liniger (1978, 1979) — contractive methods for stiff differential equations, parts I and II — the joint construction used in Sect. V.6.
- Nevanlinna & Odeh (1981) — multiplier techniques for linear multistep methods — produces the [[concepts/multiplier-technique]] (subject index 342f) framework.
- Jeltsch-Nevanlinna theorem (subject index 174 / 631) — largest disk of stability of explicit Runge-Kutta methods (Jeltsch & Nevanlinna 1978, 1981, 1982).

## Common Strategies

- Multiplier techniques for stability proofs of multistep methods on nonlinear problems.
- Logarithmic-norm analysis for one-sided Lipschitz problems.

## Related Entities
