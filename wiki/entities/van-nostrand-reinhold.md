## Overview

Van Nostrand Reinhold Company is the publisher of *Computer Methods for Circuit Analysis and Design* by Vlach and Singhal (1983), as part of its Electrical/Computer Science and Engineering Series edited by Sanjit Mitra. Headquartered in New York with offices in Cincinnati, Toronto, London, and Melbourne.

## Characteristics

- Series: Van Nostrand Reinhold Electrical/Computer Science and Engineering Series.
- Series editor (at time of publication): Sanjit Mitra.
- Companion titles in series cover programmable-calculator design, compiler design, microwave semiconductor engineering, quartz crystal design, digital image processing, software testing, VLSI hardware/software, and modeling dynamic systems.

## Common Strategies

- Publisher of engineering and computer-science textbooks aimed at upper-undergraduate and graduate audiences.
