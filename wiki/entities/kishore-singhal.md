## Overview

Kishore Singhal is a professor in the Department of Systems Design at the University of Waterloo, Waterloo, Ontario, Canada. He is co-author (with Jiri Vlach) of *Computer Methods for Circuit Analysis and Design* (Van Nostrand Reinhold, 1983), one of the foundational textbooks on computer-aided circuit analysis and design.

## Characteristics

- Affiliation: Department of Systems Design, University of Waterloo.
- Research areas implicit in the book: CAD, sparse matrices, numerical integration of algebraic-differential systems, optimization-based design, sensitivity analysis.
- Contributed to WATAND, the Waterloo Analysis and Design interactive CAD package.

## Common Strategies

- Modern (for 1983) formulation methods that produce sparse algebraic-differential systems.
- Adjoint-based sensitivity computation as the workhorse for gradients used in optimization.
- Use of sequential quadratic programming for constrained optimization in network design.
