## Overview

Martin Kleppmann is the author of *Designing Data-Intensive Applications* (O'Reilly, 2017) and a distributed-systems researcher at the University of Cambridge, UK. Before academia, he was a software engineer and entrepreneur at LinkedIn and Rapportive (acquired by LinkedIn), where he worked on large-scale data infrastructure. He is a regular conference speaker, blogger, and contributor to open-source projects, with a stated goal of making deep technical ideas accessible.

## Characteristics

- Author of *Designing Data-Intensive Applications*, widely regarded as a definitive practitioner-oriented treatment of modern data systems.
- Research interests include CRDTs, local-first software, end-to-end-encrypted collaboration, and consensus protocols.
- Industrial background at LinkedIn (Kafka, Espresso, Samza ecosystem) and Rapportive.

## Common Strategies

- Synthesizes academic research and industrial folklore in writing and talks.
- Advocates for principled understanding of trade-offs over buzzword-driven tool selection.
