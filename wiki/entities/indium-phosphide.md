## Overview

Indium phosphide is a III-V compound semiconductor with a direct bandgap of 1.35 eV. Together with lattice-matched alloys (InGaAs and InGaAsP), it is the workhorse substrate for telecom-wavelength (1.3 and 1.55 um) lasers, photodetectors, and high-speed transistors.

## Characteristics

- Bandgap: 1.35 eV (direct, room temperature).
- Lattice constant: 5.87 A (zincblende).
- Electron mobility ~5400 cm^2/V-s (pure, 300 K).
- Lattice-matched to InGaAs (Eg ~ 0.75 eV) and InGaAsP alloys spanning 0.75-1.35 eV bandgap.
- Semi-insulating (Fe-doped) wafers available for high-speed IC substrates.

## Common Strategies

- Telecom-wavelength laser diodes for fiber-optic networks.
- Photodetectors (PIN, APD) for 1.55 um receivers.
- InP-based HEMTs and HBTs for mm-wave and terahertz.
- Quantum-cascade lasers in InGaAs/InAlAs system.
