## Overview

Indium phosphide is a III-V compound semiconductor. Sze & Ng's Appendix E lists InP: zincblende (Z) crystal structure, lattice constant 5.8686 Å, indirect/direct gap 1.35 eV / 1.42 eV (D = direct), electron mobility 4600 cm²/V·s, hole mobility 150 cm²/V·s, electron effective mass 0.077, hole effective mass 0.64, relative permittivity 12.6. Together with lattice-matched alloys (InGaAs and InGaAsP), it is the workhorse substrate for telecom-wavelength (1.3 and 1.55 µm) lasers, photodetectors, and high-speed transistors discussed in Sze Sect. 12.

## Characteristics

- Bandgap: 1.35 eV (direct, room temperature; per Appendix E).
- Lattice constant: 5.8686 Å (zincblende, per Appendix E).
- Electron mobility 4600 cm²/V·s (Appendix E).
- Lattice-matched to InGaAs (Eg ~0.75 eV) and InGaAsP alloys spanning 0.75-1.35 eV bandgap.
- Semi-insulating (Fe-doped) wafers available for high-speed IC substrates.

## Common Strategies

- Telecom-wavelength laser diodes for fiber-optic networks.
- Photodetectors (PIN, APD) for 1.55 um receivers.
- InP-based HEMTs and HBTs for mm-wave and terahertz.
- Quantum-cascade lasers in InGaAs/InAlAs system.
