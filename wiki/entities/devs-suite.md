## Overview

DEVS-Suite is the simulator within the CoSMoS family, built on DEVSJAVA and the DEVS Tracking Environment. It supports parallel DEVS atomic/coupled models, animation, time-trajectory views, design-of-experiments, and data logging. It is the host for the SOAD framework's service-based simulation models.

## Characteristics

- DEVSJAVA-based
- Configurable synchronized control for simulation and viewing
- Built-in transducers for QoS metric collection
- Used in academic and government settings worldwide
- Hosted at http://acims.asu.edu

## Common Strategies

- Validating SOA-compliant service compositions (Travel Agent, Voice Communication)
- Hierarchical service composition modeling
- Animation of message flow alongside time-trajectory plots
