## Overview

The Software Engineering Institute (SEI) at Carnegie Mellon University is a federally funded research and development center sponsored by the U.S. Department of Defense. It conducts research in software architecture, secure systems, and large-scale distributed computing. Ian Gorton led big-data and massively scalable systems R&D at the SEI before joining Northeastern.

## Characteristics

- Located in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania.
- Has published influential frameworks including CMMI and architectural quality-attribute methods.
- Collaborates with industry and government on software-systems practice.

## Common Strategies

- Combines academic rigor with practitioner-focused tools and reports.
