## Overview

Gerhard Wanner is a numerical analyst at the Université de Genève and co-author with [[entities/ernst-hairer]] of both volumes of "Solving Ordinary Differential Equations" (Vols. I, II in the Springer Series in Computational Mathematics). With [[entities/syvert-norsett]] he is also a Volume I co-author.

## Characteristics

- Co-developer of the order-stars theory (used in Sects. IV.4 and V.3–V.4 to prove A-stability barriers, the Daniel-Moore conjecture, and the dual order-stars used for SDIRK methods).
- The cover figure of the Springer Series in Computational Mathematics is "based on Figure 10A on page 149 of this book" (front-matter) — Wanner's order-star diagram.
- Cited throughout the book's W-transformation theory (Sect. IV.5, IV.8) — the matrix-decomposition device that makes implicit Runge-Kutta linear-algebra tractable.

## Common Strategies

- Order-stars and Padé approximation analysis for A-, L-, and B-stability.
- W-transformation simplification of IRK linear-algebra systems (used in [[entities/radau5]]).
