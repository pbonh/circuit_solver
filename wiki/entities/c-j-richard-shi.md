## Overview

C.-J. Richard Shi is a researcher at the University of Washington credited by Shi/Tan/Tlelo-Cuautle as the source of inspiration for many of the foundational ideas in the Advanced Symbolic Analysis for VLSI Systems monograph, particularly determinant decision diagrams.

## Characteristics

- Originator (with collaborators) of the Determinant Decision Diagram (DDD) line of work.
- Active in mixed-signal CAD and symbolic analysis research.

## Common Strategies

- Cited as a primary reference for early DDD literature.
