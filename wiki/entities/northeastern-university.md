## Overview

Northeastern University is a private research university headquartered in Boston, Massachusetts, with regional campuses including Seattle. Its Khoury College of Computer Sciences hosts the CS6650 "Building Scalable Distributed Systems" graduate course taught by Ian Gorton.

## Characteristics

- Multiple campuses across North America and Europe.
- Known for cooperative-education (co-op) program.
- The Seattle campus offers MS programs in computer science.

## Common Strategies

- Industry-aligned curriculum.
- Strong cooperative-education ties to major tech employers.
