## Overview

Esteban Tlelo-Cuautle is a researcher at INAOE (Mexico) and a co-author of Advanced Symbolic Analysis for VLSI Systems. He contributed the chapter on nullor-based symbolic nodal analysis of analog circuits.

## Characteristics

- CONACyT project 131839 funded his contribution.
- Long-running collaboration with Sheldon X.-D. Tan (UC MEXUS-CONACYT grant CN-11-575).
- Research interests: nullor/pathological-element-based analog modeling, symbolic analysis of active filters.

## Common Strategies

- Reduced-dimensional MNA via nullor equivalents.
- Behavioral-level modeling of active analog blocks before symbolic expansion.
