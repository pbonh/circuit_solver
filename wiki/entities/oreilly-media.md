## Overview

O'Reilly Media is an American technical book and online learning publisher based in Sebastopol, California, founded by Tim O'Reilly. It publishes many foundational computing texts, including Foundations of Scalable Systems by Ian Gorton (2022).

## Characteristics

- Located at 1005 Gravenstein Highway North, Sebastopol, CA 95472.
- Operates the O'Reilly Online Learning platform (formerly Safari Books).
- Known for animal-illustrated covers (Foundations of Scalable Systems features a dusky grouper).

## Common Strategies

- Bridges technical books, video courses, and live training under one subscription.
- Curates content across software engineering, data, security, and business.
