## Overview

Aaron Turon is a long-time Rust contributor and co-author of the foreword to *The Rust Programming Language*. He has worked on Rust's concurrency story and the design of safe concurrency primitives.

## Characteristics

- Co-author of the foreword to the official Rust book
- Known for work on Rust's concurrency and library design
