## Overview

CoSMoS (Component-Based System Modeler and Simulator) is an integrated platform for developing component-based, modular, hierarchical families of models. It is grounded in a unified logical, visual, and persistent perspective on models, and supports parallel DEVS, Cellular Automata, and XML Schema model families. DEVS-Suite is the bundled simulator (built on DEVSJAVA and the DEVS Tracking Environment) that runs the models with configurable synchronized control. Both are hosted at http://acims.asu.edu.

## Characteristics

- Models stored in relational databases for persistence
- Model complexity metrics built in
- Separates simulatable from non-simulatable models
- Supports software/hardware system architecture composition for SoS
- DEVS-Suite supports parallel DEVS, time-trajectory views, animation, data logging

## Common Strategies

- Used to model semiconductor supply-chain manufacturing, service-oriented computing, MIPS32 processors, Network-on-Chip, swarm net, UML design patterns, household agents
- Adopted by academic institutions and government/commercial research centers internationally
- Featured in Chapters 14–16 of the Zeigler/Sarjoughian guide
- Drives design-of-experiments combined with animated simulation execution
