## Overview

Ernst Hairer is a numerical analyst at the Université de Genève, Section de Mathématiques, 2-4 rue du Lièvre (front-matter contact). With Gerhard Wanner he is the lead author of "Solving Ordinary Differential Equations II — Stiff and Differential-Algebraic Problems" (Springer Series in Computational Mathematics 14, 2nd revised edition 1996). The pair are co-authors of Volume I together with [[entities/syvert-norsett]].

## Characteristics

- Springer Series in Computational Mathematics, Vol. 14 main author.
- Bibliography author/co-author of foundational papers on A- and B-stability characterization (1980, 1982, 1986), backward analysis of numerical integrators / symplectic methods (1994), and (with Wanner / Lubich / Roche) the index-1 and index-2 DAE Runge-Kutta theory used in the book's Sect. VI.4–VII.6.
- Co-author with Ostermann on dense-output for extrapolation methods (1990).
- Distributor of the Fortran reference codes [[entities/radau5]], [[entities/rodas]], [[entities/seulex]], [[entities/sdirk4]], [[entities/dopri5]] in the book's Appendix.

## Common Strategies

- Implicit Runge-Kutta theory for stiff and DAE problems, including the Radau IIA L-stable family (Sect. IV.5, IV.8).
- Rosenbrock-method error analysis for DAEs (with Lubich and Roche, 1988-1989).
- Symplectic integrators for constrained Hamiltonian systems (Sect. VII.8).
