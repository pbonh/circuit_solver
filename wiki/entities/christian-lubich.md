## Overview

Christian (Ch.) Lubich is a numerical analyst (Tübingen) and long-time collaborator of the Geneva group. The book's preface places him at the centre of the Geneva seminar where "all the subjects of this book have been presented and discussed over the years"; both editions credit him with reading the whole text. His own bibliography in the book is one of the densest of any author — about a dozen entries spanning extrapolation at stiff DAEs, half-explicit DAE methods, backward error analysis, and convolution quadrature.

## Characteristics

- Hairer, Bader & Lubich (1982) — stability of semi-implicit methods for parabolic problems.
- Hairer & Lubich (1988a, 1988b) — extrapolation at stiff and differential-algebraic equations; the theoretical foundation for [[entities/seulex]] and [[entities/sodex]].
- Hairer, Lubich & Roche (1988, 1989) — error of Runge-Kutta and Rosenbrock methods for stiff DAE problems (the "HLR89" lecture notes referenced via the HLR89 index entry).
- Lubich (1989, 1993) — MEXX extrapolation code for constrained mechanical systems; analysis of highly oscillatory regimes for stringent-tolerance DAE integration (Sect. VII.7).
- Lubich & Ostermann (1993) — Runge-Kutta methods for parabolic equations and convolution quadrature.
- Hairer & Lubich (1996) — life-span of backward error analysis for numerical integrators (the foundation for the book's symplectic methods chapter Sect. VII.8).

## Common Strategies

- Convolution-quadrature analysis for stiff and parabolic problems.
- Half-explicit and extrapolation methods for index-2 constrained mechanical systems.
