## Overview

Jiri Vlach is a professor of Electrical Engineering at the University of Waterloo, Waterloo, Ontario, Canada. He is co-author (with Kishore Singhal) of *Computer Methods for Circuit Analysis and Design* (Van Nostrand Reinhold, 1983), a foundational CAD textbook covering circuit theory, formulation methods, sensitivity, numerical Laplace inversion, nonlinear analysis, and optimization.

## Characteristics

- Affiliation: Department of Electrical Engineering, University of Waterloo.
- Research areas implicit in the book: linear and nonlinear circuit analysis, sparse-matrix techniques, sensitivities, numerical Laplace transform inversion, switched-capacitor networks, CAD software.
- Contributed to WATAND (Waterloo Analysis and Design), the interactive CAD package developed at Waterloo.

## Common Strategies

- Frequency- and time-domain network analysis using modified nodal and tableau formulations.
- Use of numerical Laplace inversion for time-domain linear analysis (a recurring technique in the book).
- Pedagogical emphasis on developing CAD knowledge so that practitioners can modify or extend simulation software.
