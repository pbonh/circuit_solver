## Overview

VLE (Virtual Laboratory Environment) is a DEVS-based modeling and simulation platform aimed at collaborative development of simulation models for Living Systems of Systems. It combines traditional continuous models with discrete-event and other modern formalisms to encourage collaborative development, validation, replication, and experimentation within a virtual laboratory context. Distribution: http://vle-project.org.

## Characteristics

- Multi-formalism: continuous + discrete-event + modern formalisms within DEVS
- Designed for collaborative model libraries
- Strong support for agriculture and epidemiology
- Adopted by the French National Institute for Agricultural Research (INRA) for its RECORD project

## Common Strategies

- Used for control of epidemic outbreaks (e.g., avian flu)
- Re-engineering crop varieties (e.g., rice) for resilience
- Integration of heterogeneous existing agricultural simulation models
- Featured in Chapter 17 of the Zeigler/Sarjoughian guide
