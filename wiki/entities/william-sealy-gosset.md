## Overview

William Sealy Gosset (1876-1937) is the statistician who first published the t-distribution in 1908 under the pen name "Student." The chapter mentions him to explain why the distribution is known as Student's t-distribution.

## Characteristics

- British statistician
- Worked at Guinness brewery in Dublin
- Published under the pseudonym "Student"

## Common Strategies

- Used by analysts dealing with small samples and unknown population variance
- Underpins one-sample, two-sample, and paired t-tests in modern statistics
