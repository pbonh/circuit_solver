## Key Points

- Shankar Rao Pandala is a data scientist at Cognizant with a computer science bachelor's and a master's in financial markets; he works across finance, healthcare, manufacturing, and consulting with an AI-for-trading interest.
- Prashant Sahu holds a B.Tech from NIT Rourkela (2003) and is pursuing a doctorate at IIT Bombay in instrumentation, data analytics, modeling, and simulation applied to semiconductor materials and devices.
- Sahu is head of training services at Tech Smart Systems (Pune, India) and SVP of innovation at Bharati Robotic Systems.
- Sahu has 15+ years of experience in research, automation, simulation/modeling, data analytics, image processing, control systems, optimization, genetic algorithms, and cryptography.
- Sahu has trained faculty and corporate developers across India and is an external examiner and Syllabus Revision Committee member at the University of Pune.
