## Key Points

- The author, Padraig Houlahan, has worked across research, teaching, and IT management, and has held roles as a scientist, software developer, college professor, flight instructor, and IT director.
- Houlahan's M.A. research placed upper limits on gravitational waves using Lunar Laser Ranging data; his Ph.D. research developed a pattern-recognition approach for characterizing astronomical nebulae.
- His subsequent work has focused on aviation-centric software projects involving real-time tracking and visualization of aircraft for traffic-flow analysis.
- He has helped make extensive datasets accessible to students, faculty, and airline/airport operators.
- He emphasizes a teaching motivation rooted in the joy of seeing students gain insight from data.
