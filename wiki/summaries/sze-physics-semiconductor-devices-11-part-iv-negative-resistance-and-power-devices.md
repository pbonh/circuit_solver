## Key Points

- Part IV covers four chapters: Tunnel Devices (Ch. 8), IMPATT Diodes (Ch. 9), Transferred-Electron and Real-Space-Transfer Devices (Ch. 10), and Thyristors and Power Devices (Ch. 11).
- The unifying theme is devices that either exhibit negative differential resistance (tunnel diodes, IMPATT, transferred-electron) -- and hence can generate microwave power or function as switching elements with few components -- or carry very large currents and block large voltages (thyristors, IGBTs, power MOSFETs).
