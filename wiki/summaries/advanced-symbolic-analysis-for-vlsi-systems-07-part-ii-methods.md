## Key Points

- Part II divider page; only the section title "Methods" appears.
- Part II covers DDD (Chap. 4), DDD implementation via LED (Chap. 5), generalized two-graph theory (Chap. 6), GPDD (Chap. 7), hierarchical analysis (Chap. 8), and nullor-based reduced MNA symbolic analysis (Chap. 9).
