## Key Points

- Volume II treats stiff differential equations and differential-algebraic equations (DAEs); it is split into Chapter IV (one-step / Runge–Kutta methods for stiff problems), Chapter V (multistep methods for stiff problems), Chapter VI (singular perturbation and DAEs), and (in the 2nd edition) Chapter VII (DAEs of higher index).
- The first sections of each chapter are introductory — explaining numerical phenomena and showing numerical results — while later sections present theoretical investigations.
- Notation: formulas, theorems, tables and figures are numbered consecutively within each section; chapter cross-references use Latin chapter numbers; bibliography entries are referenced by "author (year)" and the bibliography only lists papers actually discussed.
- Acknowledged contributors include J. Butcher, G. Dahlquist, S.P. Nørsett (coauthor of Volume I), and members of the Geneva seminar — Ch. Lubich, A. Ostermann, M. Roche — plus many readers and reviewers.
- Second-edition additions: methods with extended stability (Chebyshev methods, Sect. IV.2); improved codes and numerical tests (Sects. IV.10 and V.5); new results on error growth functions (Sects. IV.11–IV.12); quasilinear ODEs with state-dependent mass matrix (Sect. VI.6).
- The DAE chapter was completely reorganized to include index reduction (Sect. VII.2), half-explicit methods for index-2 systems (Sect. VII.6), and symplectic methods for constrained Hamiltonian systems with backward error analysis on manifolds (Sect. VII.8).
- All figures were recomputed and rendered, together with text, in PostScript; the manuscript was prepared in TeX (Knuth) and produced camera-ready.
- First-edition preface dated March 1991; second-edition preface dated April 1996.
