## Key Points

- Web-deployed dashboards solve the perennial problem of distributing data tools across heterogeneous student/colleague OS environments — everyone visits a URL instead of installing software.
- For very-high-demand dashboards, scaling and load-balancing technologies like Kubernetes (or equivalent Google Cloud offerings) are available, though most small project teams will not need them.
- Divide-and-conquer: prefer multiple focused dashboards exposed through a web portal over a single mega-dashboard; this gives users a menu of stable tools rather than a moving target.
- Stand-alone dashboards self-document and stabilize a team's accumulated expertise, freeing developers to take on new challenges.
- The author frames dashboards as the natural next step beyond command-line scripts, and as an effective vehicle for teaching students how to communicate technical results with compelling graphics.
