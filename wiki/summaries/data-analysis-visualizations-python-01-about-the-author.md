## Key Points

- The author, Dr. Ossama Embarak, holds a PhD in computer and information science from Heriot-Watt University in Scotland, UK.
- He has more than two decades of research and teaching experience across multiple programming languages including C++, Java, C#, R, and Python.
- He is the lead CIS program coordinator at Higher Colleges of Technology in the UAE, serving over 23,000 students.
- He received a 199,000 interdisciplinary research grant to build a machine learning system for mining student knowledge and skills.
- He has published widely in data mining, online privacy, semantic web, and knowledge discovery and reviews/edits journals on AI, data mining, machine learning, and mobile/web technologies.
