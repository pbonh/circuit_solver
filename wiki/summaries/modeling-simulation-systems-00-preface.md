## Key Points

- The book targets "Systems of Systems" (SoS) problems where direct real-world experimentation is dangerous, costly, unethical, or risky, motivating a Virtual Build and Test methodology.
- Two formalisms anchor the book: Discrete-Event Systems Specification (DEVS) for simulation modeling and System Entity Structure (SES) as a high-level ontology for model composition.
- Commercial and open-source DEVS Modeling and Simulation Environments (MS4 Me, CoSMoS/DEVS-Suite, VLE) are covered in depth.
- A signature contribution: integrating both energy and information processing requirements into system design via DEVS "activity" concepts that emulate biological balancing of computation against resource use.
- This edition adds a new chapter on DEVS Support for Markov Modeling and Simulation, plus expansions of Chapters 8 and 16 on SoS modeling guidance.
- Authors: Bernard P. Zeigler (Potomac, USA) and Hessam S. Sarjoughian (Tempe, USA), with Chapter 17 co-authored by Raphaël Duboz and Jean-Christophe Soulié.
