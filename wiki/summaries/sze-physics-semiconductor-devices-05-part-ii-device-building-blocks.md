## Key Points

- Part II introduces the three elementary semiconductor structures from which all useful devices can be built: p-n junctions (Chapter 2), metal-semiconductor (Schottky/ohmic) contacts (Chapter 3), and the metal-insulator-semiconductor (MIS) capacitor (Chapter 4).
- Together these building blocks define the carrier-flow boundary conditions that govern every transistor, diode, optoelectronic device, sensor, and power switch in the rest of the book.
