## Key Points

- Volume II of the canonical Hairer–Wanner two-volume series on solving ordinary differential equations.
- Subtitle: *Stiff and Differential-Algebraic Problems*; published as Springer Series in Computational Mathematics, vol. 14.
- Authors: E. Hairer and G. Wanner of the Section de Mathematiques at Universite de Geneve.
- Second revised edition (1996), with corrected second printing (2002), softcover reprint of the hardcover 2nd edition.
- ISBN 978-3-642-05220-0 (print) and 978-3-642-05221-7 (eBook); DOI 10.1007/978-3-642-05221-7.
- Mathematics Subject Classification (2000): 65Lxx (numerical methods for ODEs) and 34A50 (DAEs and equations on manifolds).
- The cover design draws from Figure 10A on page 149 (a stability-domain figure).
- Dedicated "To Evi and Myriam".
