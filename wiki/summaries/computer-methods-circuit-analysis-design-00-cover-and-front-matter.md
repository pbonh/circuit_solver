## Key Points

- The book is titled *Computer Methods for Circuit Analysis and Design*, authored by Jiri Vlach (Department of Electrical Engineering) and Kishore Singhal (Department of Systems Design), both at the University of Waterloo, Waterloo, Ontario, Canada.
- Published 1983 by Van Nostrand Reinhold Company (New York) within the Van Nostrand Reinhold Electrical/Computer Science and Engineering Series edited by Sanjit Mitra.
- Library of Congress classification TK7867.V58 1983 (Electronic circuits; Electronic circuit design — Data processing). ISBN 0-442-28108-0.
- The front matter lists companion books in the series (e.g., handbooks on electronic design with programmable calculators, compiler design, microwave semiconductor engineering, digital image processing, VLSI hardware/software concepts).
- Intended as a graduate/advanced undergraduate text on CAD-oriented circuit analysis and design.
