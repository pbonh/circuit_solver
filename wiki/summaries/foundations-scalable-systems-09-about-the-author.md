## Key Points
- Ian Gorton has 30 years' experience as a software architect, professor, and consultant, focused on distributed technologies since graduate school.
- Has worked on large-scale software systems in banking, telecommunications, government, healthcare, and scientific modeling/simulation.
- Author of three books, including "Essential Software Architecture" and "Data Intensive Computing," and over 200 scientific/professional publications.
- Led R&D in big data and massively scalable systems at the CMU Software Engineering Institute; joined Northeastern University as CS professor in 2015.
- Holds a PhD from Sheffield Hallam University, UK; senior member of the IEEE Computer Society.
- Colophon notes the cover animal is the dusky grouper (Epinephelus marginatus), a vulnerable species.
