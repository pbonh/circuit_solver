## Key Points

- Appendix F is a brief catalog of community translations of *The Rust Programming Language*. Most translations are works in progress.
- Languages listed: Portuguese (BR and PT), Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Ukrainian, Spanish, Italian, Russian, Korean, Japanese, French, Polish, Cebuano, Tagalog, Esperanto, Greek, Swedish, Persian (Farsi), German, Hindi, Thai, and Danish.
- The Rust project tracks translation work under a "Translations" issue label on its repository and welcomes new translators.
