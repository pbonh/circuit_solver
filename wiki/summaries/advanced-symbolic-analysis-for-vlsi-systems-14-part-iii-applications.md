## Key Points

- Part III divider page; only the section title "Applications" appears.
- Part III applies the symbolic methods of Parts I-II to three VLSI-design pain-points: large mesh-interconnect moment computation (Chap. 10), worst-case performance bound analysis under process variation (Chap. 11), and GPU-parallel statistical Monte Carlo (Chap. 12).
- Common thread: process variation in nanometer technologies makes statistical / variational analysis the dominant cost, and symbolic methods are positioned as the acceleration enabler.
