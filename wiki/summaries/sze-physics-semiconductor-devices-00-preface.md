## Key Points

- Physics of Semiconductor Devices is one of the most-cited engineering texts (>15,000 citations as of 2006) and is the standard reference for device physicists, engineers, and graduate students.
- The Third Edition (2006) revises or updates over 50% of the Second Edition (1981) and reorganizes the material into five parts: semiconductor physics, device building blocks, transistors, negative-resistance and power devices, and photonic devices and sensors.
- New material added for the Third Edition includes three-dimensional MOSFETs, nonvolatile memory, modulation-doped FETs (MODFETs), single-electron transistors, resonant-tunneling diodes, insulated-gate bipolar transistors (IGBTs), quantum cascade lasers, and semiconductor sensors.
- The book serves as both a graduate textbook and a research reference, with end-of-chapter problem sets and a solutions manual.
- Authors: S. M. Sze (National Chiao Tung University / National Nano Device Laboratories, Hsinchu, Taiwan) and Kwok K. Ng (Agere Systems / MVC, San Jose, CA).
