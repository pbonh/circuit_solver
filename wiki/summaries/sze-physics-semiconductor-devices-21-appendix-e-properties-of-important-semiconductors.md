## Key Points

- The appendix is a compact table of physical and electrical properties of more than twenty important elemental and compound semiconductors at 300 K, plus a more detailed parameter list for Si and GaAs (Appendix G).
- Tabulated quantities: crystal structure (D = diamond, Z = zincblende, W = wurtzite, R = rock-salt), lattice constant, bandgap at 300 K and 0 K, direct or indirect (D/I), electron and hole mobility, electron and hole effective masses, dielectric constant.
- Group-IV: C (diamond, Eg = 5.47 eV indirect, mu_n = 1800), Ge (Eg = 0.66 eV indirect, mu_n = 3900), Si (Eg = 1.12 eV indirect, mu_n = 1450).
- IV-IV: SiC (3.0 eV indirect, wurtzite-like with multiple polytypes).
- III-V: AlAs, AlP, AlSb, BN, BP, GaAs (1.42 eV direct, mu_n = 8000), GaN (3.44 eV direct), GaP (2.26 eV indirect), GaSb (0.72 eV direct), InAs (0.36 eV direct, mu_n = 33000), InP (1.35 eV direct, mu_n = 4600), InSb (0.17 eV direct, mu_n = 80000).
- II-VI: CdS, CdSe, CdTe, ZnO, ZnS.
- IV-VI: PbS (0.41 eV indirect), PbTe (0.31 eV indirect).
- For Si and GaAs (Appendix G), a more complete property list is given: atomic density, effective densities of states Nc, Nv, intrinsic carrier density (9.65e9 cm^-3 for Si, 2.1e6 for GaAs), drift mobilities, saturation velocity (1e7 cm/s for Si, 7e6 for GaAs), thermal conductivity, breakdown field, etc.
- This appendix is the single most-cited table in the field for semiconductor material parameters and provides the numerical inputs needed for the device calculations developed in Chapters 1-14.
