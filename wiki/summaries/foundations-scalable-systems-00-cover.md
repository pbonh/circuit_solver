## Key Points
- "Foundations of Scalable Systems: Designing Distributed Architectures" by Ian Gorton, O'Reilly Media, ISBN 978-1-098-10606-5.
- Targets software architects and developers building internet-facing systems that must scale; covers replication, state management, load balancing, caching, asynchronous messaging, serverless, and microservices.
- Distinct chapters address scalable databases (NoSQL, eventual vs. strong consistency) and event-driven and stream-processing systems (e.g., Kafka, Flink).
- Author Ian Gorton has 30+ years' experience as a software architect, CMU SEI researcher, and now Northeastern University CS professor; also wrote "Essential Software Architecture" and "Data Intensive Computing".
- Praise blurbs frame the book around trade-off thinking for cloud-era distributed systems; recommended for both students and practitioners.
