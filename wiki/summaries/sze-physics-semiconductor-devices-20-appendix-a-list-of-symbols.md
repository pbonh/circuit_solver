## Key Points

- Appendix A is a reference table of the symbols used throughout the book, with their physical interpretation and unit, organized roughly alphabetically.
- Covered categories include: energies (E_c, E_v, E_F, E_Fn, E_Fp, E_g, E_i, E_t, E_p), fields (E electric field, E_c critical, E_m maximum), frequencies and gains (f, f_T, f_max, F-D distributions, g_m, g_p), currents (I_s, I_F, I_H, I_n, I_p, I_ph, etc.) and current densities (J_s, J_F, J_n, J_p, etc.), constants (k, h, h-bar, q), lengths (L, L_d Debye length, diffusion lengths L_n, L_p), masses (m_e* effective mass and various subscripts), and so on.
- The unit conventions follow standard SI / CGS-as-used in textbooks (cm-based, eV-based where appropriate).
