## Key Points

- Dr. Mingyu Xiao is a professor in the School of Computer Science and Engineering at the University of Electronic Science and Technology of China (Chengdu).
- Mingyu Xiao received his PhD in Computer Science from the Chinese University of Hong Kong in 2008.
- Dr. Ton Kloks is a researcher in graph algorithms; he studied mathematics at Eindhoven University (Netherlands) during the 1980s.
- Ton Kloks received his PhD in Computer Science from Utrecht University (Netherlands) in 1993, with a thesis on treewidth.
- The author profiles establish both authors as long-standing contributors to graph algorithm research, with deep ties to treewidth and parameterized algorithms.
