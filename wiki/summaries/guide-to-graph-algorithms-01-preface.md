## Key Points

- The book originated from summer schools at the University of Science and Technology of China; a first draft was prepared in 2016-2017 and substantially updated in subsequent years.
- It provides an introduction to the research area of graph algorithms and reviews developments over the last decade.
- Contents are divided into four parts: "Graphs" (basic graph theoretic concepts), "Algorithms" (early highlights in graph algorithms), "Problem Formulations" (graph algebras and monadic second-order logic), and "Recent Trends" (uses treewidth as a stepping stone to discuss recent developments).
- Treewidth is positioned as the central organizing concept for the "Recent Trends" portion of the book.
- The book assumes familiarity with first-year computer science algorithms material and contains many research-level exercises.
- The text serves as a smooth on-ramp for those who want to dive deep into graph algorithms research.
