## Key Points

- Part V covers semiconductor devices that interface with non-electrical signal domains: photonic emitters and detectors (Chapters 12 and 13) and sensors for thermal, mechanical, magnetic, and chemical signals (Chapter 14).
- Chapter 12 (LEDs and Lasers): radiative recombination, LED structures, semiconductor laser physics and operating characteristics, specialty lasers (quantum-well, VCSEL, quantum cascade).
- Chapter 13 (Photodetectors and Solar Cells): photoconductors, photodiodes (p-n, p-i-n, heterojunction, Schottky), avalanche photodiodes, phototransistors, CCD and CMOS image sensors, MSM detectors, QWIPs, and solar cells.
- Chapter 14 (Sensors): semiconductor implementations of thermal, mechanical (strain, SAW), magnetic (Hall, magnetoresistor, magnetotransistor), and chemical (MOS gas sensor, ISFET, biosensors) sensors.
