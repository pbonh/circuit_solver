## Key Points

- Martin Kleppmann is a distributed-systems researcher at the University of Cambridge, UK.
- Prior to academia, he was a software engineer and entrepreneur at internet companies including LinkedIn and Rapportive, where he worked on large-scale data infrastructure.
- He is a regular conference speaker, blogger, and open-source contributor, motivated by making profound technical ideas accessible.
- The colophon describes the cover animal (the Indian wild boar, *Sus scrofa cristatus*) and notes the fonts used — Adobe Minion Pro for body text, URW Typewriter and Guardian Sans for the cover, Adobe Myriad and Dalton Maag Ubuntu Mono for diagrams and code.
