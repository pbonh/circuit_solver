## Key Points

- Rassul Bairamkulov was born in Karaganda, Kazakhstan in 1994; B.Eng. in EEE from Nazarbayev University (2016), M.S. and Ph.D. in EE from the University of Rochester (Ph.D. 2022 under Prof. Eby G. Friedman).
- Bairamkulov interned with the Power Design team at Qualcomm in 2018 and 2020; his research interests include graph theory, physical design of integrated circuits, and EDA for conventional and emerging VLSI technologies.
- Eby G. Friedman holds a B.S. from Lafayette College and M.S./Ph.D. from UC Irvine; was with Hughes Aircraft (1979-1991) leading Signal Processing Design and Test, then joined the University of Rochester in 1991 where he is a Distinguished Professor and Director of the High Performance VLSI/IC Design and Analysis Laboratory.
- Friedman is a Visiting Professor at Technion-Israel Institute of Technology; has authored 600+ articles/book chapters; authored/edited 20 books on high-speed and low-power CMOS, 3D design, high-speed interconnect, superconductive circuits, and synchronous clock/power distribution networks; holds 26 patents.
- Friedman's research interests center on high-performance synchronous digital and mixed-signal circuit design for processors, low-power wireless, and server farms.
- Honors include IEEE Circuits and Systems Mac Van Valkenburg Award, Charles A. Desoer Technical Achievement Award, EIC of IEEE Trans. VLSI Systems and Microelectronics Journal, Senior Fulbright Fellow, and UC Irvine Engineering Hall of Fame.
