## Key Points

- The book grew out of a one-week course on graph algorithmics that Mingyu Xiao invited Ton Kloks to give to second- and third-year students at the University of Electronic Science and Technology of China.
- The first draft of the text was written in 2016-2017 and substantially updated in the summers of 2018, 2019, 2020, and 2021.
- Kloks thanks his wife, Mingyu Xiao, and the University of Electronic Science and Technology of China for the opportunity.
- The acknowledgments establish the pedagogical origin of the material as a graduate-level introductory course.
