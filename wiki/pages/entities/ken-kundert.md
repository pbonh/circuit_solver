# Ken Kundert

Dr. Ken Kundert is a fellow at Cadence Design Systems. For many years he was the principal architect of the Spectre circuit simulation family, leading development of Spectre, SpectreHDL, and SpectreRF.

## Key facts

- Ph.D., M.Eng., B.S. in Electrical Engineering and Computer Sciences from UC Berkeley (1989, 1983, 1979)
- Played a key role in developing Hewlett-Packard's harmonic balance simulator
- Made substantial contributions to both Verilog-AMS and VHDL-AMS language standards
- Author of two books on circuit simulation: *Steady-State Methods for Simulating Analog and Microwave Circuits* (1990) and *The Designer's Guide to SPICE and Spectre* (1995)
- Introduced the KCL check (vs. SPICE's ΔI check) in Spectre, eliminating false convergence
- Introduced LTE control in voltage domain (vs. charge domain), improving accuracy on stiff circuits
- Delivered BCTM 1998 tutorial on circuit simulation (source: [[simulation-analog-mixed-signal-circuits]])
