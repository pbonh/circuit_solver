## Definition

Data science is an interdisciplinary practice combining programming, statistics, and domain knowledge to extract knowledge and insights from data. The book characterizes a data science practitioner as proficient in programming, data preparation, visualization, and statistics.

## How It Works

A data scientist typically progresses through the data lifecycle — collection, warehousing, cleaning, exploration, modeling (descriptive, predictive, prescriptive), and communication. The technical reviewer's biography lists activities spanning data warehousing, BI, analytical tooling, predictive modeling, and consulting.

## Key Parameters

- Programming proficiency (e.g., Python)
- Statistical literacy
- Data engineering / warehousing
- Domain knowledge

## When To Use

- Building predictive systems
- Strategic analytics programs
- Product development driven by data insights

## Risks & Pitfalls

- Ignoring data quality and bias
- Optimizing model metrics that do not align with business value
- Communication gaps with stakeholders
