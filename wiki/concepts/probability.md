## Definition

Probability is the mathematical study of uncertainty, quantifying how likely events are to occur. The third section of the book introduces probability as a foundation for hypothesis testing and other inferential techniques.

## How It Works

Probability builds from a sample space, events, and a measure assigning likelihoods between 0 and 1. Combinatorial reasoning (permutations and combinations) supplies counts; Bayes' theorem updates beliefs based on evidence. These foundations underpin probability distributions, sampling, and hypothesis tests.

## Key Parameters

- Sample space and event definitions
- Probability mass / density functions
- Conditional probability
- Bayes' theorem priors and likelihoods

## When To Use

- Modeling uncertainty in measurements or outcomes
- Designing and interpreting statistical tests
- Computing risks in decision making

## Risks & Pitfalls

- Confusing conditional and joint probabilities
- Ignoring base rates (base-rate fallacy)
- Misapplying independence assumptions
