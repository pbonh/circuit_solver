## Definition

Data mining is the process of discovering patterns, anomalies, and relationships within large datasets using methods from statistics, machine learning, and database systems. It is one of the author's major research and publication areas.

## How It Works

Practitioners apply algorithms such as clustering, association rules, classification, regression, and outlier detection to structured or semi-structured data, often after substantial cleaning and preprocessing. Results are validated and translated into actionable insights or models.

## Key Parameters

- Data preprocessing and feature selection
- Algorithm choice (clustering, classification, association mining)
- Evaluation against held-out data and domain knowledge
- Scalability to large datasets

## When To Use

- Knowledge discovery from large operational or research datasets
- Customer segmentation, fraud detection, web-usage analysis
- Educational analytics such as mining student knowledge profiles

## Risks & Pitfalls

- Spurious patterns from multiple-comparison effects
- Privacy concerns when mining personal data
- Garbage-in, garbage-out when source data is poor quality
